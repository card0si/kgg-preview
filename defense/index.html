Defense Solutions

Defense work comes with requirements most commercial companies were never built for. We help suppliers meet them with secure systems, clean contracting operations and a supply chain they can see.

Built for the companies behind the program

Small and mid-size manufacturers and suppliers on defense and government contracts, commercial companies entering the defense market, and teams supporting programs that need to move faster.

  • CMMC and cybersecurity readiness

    For suppliers that handle Federal Contract Information or Controlled Unclassified Information.

    We assess where you stand against NIST SP 800-171 and the CMMC level your contracts call for, then help you close the gaps and get ready for assessment.

    • Gap assessment
    • System security plan drafting
    • POA&M and remediation plan
    • Policies and procedures
    • Control implementation support
    • Assessment preparation

    We prepare you for assessment. We are not a C3PAO and do not issue certifications.

  • Export-control operations

    For teams working with ITAR- or EAR-controlled technical data.

    We build the workflows and systems behind your export compliance program: knowing what is controlled, who can see it and where it goes.

    • Data marking and handling workflows
    • Access control by person and role
    • Restricted party screening workflows
    • Recordkeeping and audit trails

    We work alongside your empowered official and legal counsel. We don't give legal advice or make jurisdiction or classification determinations.

  • Government contracting operations

    For companies selling to DoD, DLA and federal agencies.

    Find the right opportunities, respond faster and keep contract data straight once you win.

    • Opportunity tracking from SAM.gov
    • Capture and proposal support
    • Pricing and quote workflows
    • Contract deliverable tracking

  • Supply chain and production visibility

    For manufacturers running defense and commercial work on the same floor.

    Connect orders, inventory, suppliers and quality records so you can answer a program office with numbers, not estimates.

    • Supplier risk mapping
    • ERP and MRP fit review
    • Lot and traceability records
    • Production and delivery dashboards

  • Secure AI for defense suppliers

    For teams that want AI but handle sensitive data.

    Put AI to work on solicitations, technical documents and back-office tasks without moving controlled data somewhere it shouldn't go.

    • Solicitation and RFP analysis
    • Document drafting with human review
    • Deployment matched to data sensitivity
    • Usage rules and logging
