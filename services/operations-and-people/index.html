Operations and People: Make the work flow, the cash move and the team stronger.

Make the work flow, the cash move and the team stronger. This practice group covers: Workflow Design and Automation, Finance and Cash Operations, Talent and Organization Design, Mergers and Integrations.

Workflow Design and Automation

The problem: Work moves by email, spreadsheets and memory, so things get dropped.

Who this is for: Owners and leadership teams of small and mid-sized businesses that want results, not reports.

How we help

  • Workflow mapping
  • Process redesign
  • Automation build for routine tasks, approvals and handoffs
  • Standard operating procedure writing
  • Tool consolidation

What you get: Current and future workflow maps, automation build, standard operating procedures, tool plan

Typical timeline: 2 to 4 weeks for an assessment and plan; 6 to 12 weeks for build and rollout. [PLACEHOLDER: confirm standard timelines]

Example scenario (illustrative, not a real client): An office team re-keys the same order into three systems. We redesign the flow, automate the handoffs and write the procedures so it sticks.

Finance and Cash Operations

The problem: Cash is tight even when sales look good, and month-end takes too long.

Who this is for: Owners and leadership teams of small and mid-sized businesses that want results, not reports.

How we help

  • Cash flow forecasting
  • Billing and collections process
  • Close process improvement
  • Budget and planning
  • Fractional finance support

What you get: Cash forecast model, order-to-cash process map, close checklist, budget template

Typical timeline: 2 to 4 weeks for an assessment and plan; 6 to 12 weeks for build and rollout. [PLACEHOLDER: confirm standard timelines]

Example scenario (illustrative, not a real client): A growing contractor waits too long to get paid. We tighten billing, set a collections rhythm and build a weekly cash forecast.

Talent and Organization Design

The problem: Roles are unclear, hiring is slow and change does not stick.

Who this is for: Owners and leadership teams of small and mid-sized businesses that want results, not reports.

How we help

  • Organization structure
  • Roles and responsibilities
  • Hiring process design
  • Training programs
  • Change management and adoption

What you get: Organization chart, role definitions, hiring process, training plan, change plan

Typical timeline: 2 to 4 weeks for an assessment and plan; 6 to 12 weeks for build and rollout. [PLACEHOLDER: confirm standard timelines]

Example scenario (illustrative, not a real client): A company that doubled in size has no clear owners. We define the structure and roles, then build a hiring and training process to support it.

Mergers and Integrations

The problem: Deals look good on paper, then integration stalls in operations and systems.

Who this is for: Owners and leadership teams of small and mid-sized businesses that want results, not reports.

How we help

  • Pre-deal operations review
  • Integration planning
  • Systems and process integration
  • First-100-days plan

What you get: Operations review, integration roadmap, systems plan, first-100-days plan

Typical timeline: 2 to 4 weeks for an assessment and plan; 6 to 12 weeks for build and rollout. [PLACEHOLDER: confirm standard timelines]

Example scenario (illustrative, not a real client): A buyer is adding a smaller competitor. We review operations before close and run a 100-day plan to combine teams, systems and customers.
