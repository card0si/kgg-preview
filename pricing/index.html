Pricing questions

Why are prices listed as "starting at"?

Every business is different. The starting price covers the package as described. Your written quote reflects the pages, features and content you actually need.

What affects the final price?

The number of pages, how much copy we write, forms and integrations, online booking or payments, and how much existing content needs to move from an old site.

Do I have to buy a care plan?

No. Care plans are optional. You can run the site yourself, or ask us for changes as you need them.

What counts as a small edit?

Updating text, hours, prices, photos or staff details, or adding a short section to an existing page. Larger changes are quoted separately.

Can I start small and upgrade later?

Yes. You can start with a Starter site and add pages, local SEO or an AI receptionist as you grow.
