Artificial intelligence and agents that do real work

Subhead: We put AI to work on the tasks that slow your team down, with a person approving what matters.

Overview

AI is only useful when it fixes a real problem. We start with your workflows, find the work an agent can handle, build it, and train your team to run it. We are tool-agnostic and pick the model and platform that fit.

Readiness

We review your data, tools, people and risks, then score where you stand: early, developing or ready.

Use case library

  • Sales: lead research, proposal drafts, follow-up emails.
  • Operations: order intake, scheduling, status updates.
  • Finance: invoice matching, collections reminders, cash reports.
  • Customer service: first-response drafts, routing, knowledge answers.
  • Back office: document processing, data entry, report building.

Agent build and support

We design agents per workflow: what they read, what they do, when they hand off to a person. Then we build, test, launch and keep tuning.

Governance and human approval

Clear rules for data, access, review and logging. People approve every action with real consequences.

Data foundations

Agents are only as good as the data behind them. We clean, connect and secure it first.

Training and adoption

Hands-on training so teams trust and use the tools from week one.

Example scenario (illustrative, not a real client): A professional services firm spends hours on client intake. We build an agent that reads incoming requests, drafts the intake file and sends it to a staff member to approve.

Frequently asked questions

Is our data safe? We set data rules first and use tools that keep your data private. Will AI replace our staff? Our goal is to remove busywork so people focus on higher-value work. What does a first project look like? A readiness check, then one agent on one workflow, live in weeks.
