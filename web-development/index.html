How a website project runs

Five steps, with a clear owner and a check-in at each one.

  1. Discovery

    A free call about your customers, services and goals. You get a written quote.

  2. Design

    A homepage design and page plan for you to review before we build.

  3. Build

    We build every page, write or polish the copy with you and test on real phones.

  4. Launch

    Domain, HTTPS, analytics and Search Console set up, then the site goes live.

  5. Care

    Optional monthly care: hosting, updates, backups and small edits.

Typical timeline. Most small-business sites launch in 2 to 4 weeks. Larger sites with more pages or features typically take 4 to 8 weeks. The biggest factor is how quickly content and feedback come back.

Website questions

How long does a website take?

Most small-business sites launch in 2 to 4 weeks. Larger sites typically take 4 to 8 weeks. Timing depends mostly on how quickly content and feedback come back.

How much does a website cost?

Packages start at $995 for a Starter site, $2,400 for Business and $4,500 for Growth. Your written quote depends on the number of pages and features you need.

Do I need to write all the content myself?

No. Tell us about your business and we'll draft or polish the copy with you. You review and approve everything before launch.

Can you redesign my current website?

Yes. We review what you have, keep the content and pages worth keeping, and rebuild the site so it's faster and easier to use on a phone.

Will my site show up on Google?

Every site launches with page titles, descriptions, a sitemap and Search Console set up. Local SEO and Google Business Profile setup help you show up in nearby searches. No one can promise a specific ranking.

What happens after launch?

You can take the site and run it yourself, or pick a monthly care plan and we'll handle hosting, updates, backups and small edits.
