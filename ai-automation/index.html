AI & Automation

Put AI to work on the tasks that slow your team down. We start with the work, not the tool, and keep a person in charge of every decision that matters.

AI is only useful when it fixes a real problem

We start with your workflows, find the work an agent can handle, build it and train your team to run it. We're tool-agnostic and pick the model and platform that fit.

Where agents pay off first

Common starting points. Most first projects take one workflow, put one agent on it and keep a person approving the output.

  • Sales

    Lead research, proposal drafts and follow-up emails.

  • Operations

    Order intake, scheduling and status updates.

  • Finance

    Invoice matching, collections reminders and cash reports.

  • Customer service

    First-response drafts, routing and knowledge answers.

  • Back office

    Document processing, data entry and report building.

Example scenario, illustrative and not a real client

A professional services firm spends hours on client intake. We build an agent that reads incoming requests, drafts the intake file and sends it to a staff member to approve.

Questions we hear first

Is our data safe?

We set data rules first, then pick tools that keep your data private. Nothing goes into a public model's training without your written consent.

Will AI replace our staff?

The goal is to remove busywork so people focus on higher-value work. People approve every action with real consequences.

What does a first project look like?

A readiness review, then one agent on one workflow. If it earns its keep, we scale it.

Have a problem worth solving?

Tell us where things are stuck. In one 30-minute call we'll point to the gaps worth fixing first.
